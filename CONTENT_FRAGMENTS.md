
# Content Fragments Generator
## Purpose

This document defines how to generate **content fragments** used throughout the
Signals of Niana interactive experience.

Fragments are short, intelligent observations that appear throughout the
experience as floating editorial elements.

They should feel like:

• museum annotations  
• documentary notes  
• marginalia in a research notebook  
• fragments of observation  

Avoid overly sentimental language.

The tone should be:

calm  
observational  
intelligent  
respectful  

Never sound like greeting card writing.

---

# Fragment Types

## Observation Fragments

Short insights about personality, thinking style, or worldview.

Examples:

"Some people solve problems. Niana redesigns the system that created them."

"Curiosity, for her, isn't a hobby. It's infrastructure."

"Most people look for answers. She looks for patterns."

---

## Travel Fragments

Small reflections tied to movement through the world.

Examples:

"Every city rearranges the way you think."

"Movement clarifies the mind."

"Distance has a way of revealing patterns you can't see up close."

---

## Work Fragments

Observations about effort, labor, and persistence.

Examples:

"Most achievements are built from invisible hours."

"The work behind the work is where the real discipline lives."

"Consistency is a form of quiet power."

---

## Curiosity Fragments

Ideas related to systems thinking, mythology, technology, or philosophy.

Examples:

"Technology is just mythology written in code."

"Every system tells a story about what people believed was possible."

"Curiosity is the architecture of discovery."

---

# Fragment Density

The site should contain roughly:

20–40 text fragments.

These should appear gradually throughout the experience.

They should **never overwhelm the user**.

---

# Placement Strategy

Fragments should appear as:

• floating text blocks  
• side annotations  
• hovering captions near imagery  
• subtle callouts connected by lines  

They should feel like pieces of an editorial layout drifting through space.

---

# Content Expansion

The system should allow additional fragments to be easily added.

Future fragments may be pulled from:

• conversations  
• travel memories  
• journal notes  
• observations from friends
